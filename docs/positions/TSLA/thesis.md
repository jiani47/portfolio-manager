## Thesis

Bet on robotics and autonomous driving, not the car business. The EV business is mature and declining. The thesis is that Tesla is a robotics/autonomy company that happens to sell cars. FSD and Optimus are the real bets.

**What's working (Q4 2025 / FY2025 data):**
- **Robotaxi is actually launching.** Unsupervised paid rides in Austin, no chase cars as of Jan 2026. 500+ vehicle fleet, doubling monthly. Expanding to 7 more US markets (Dallas, Houston, Phoenix, Miami, Orlando, Tampa, Las Vegas) in H1 2026. This is no longer a promise — it's happening.
- **Optimus Gen 3 unveiled Q1 2026.** First design meant for mass production. Fremont S/X factory converting to Optimus production line. Target: 1M robots/yr capacity. This is ahead of most external expectations.
- **Gross margin recovered: 20.1%** (highest in 2+ years). Auto ex-credits improved from 15.4% → 17.9% sequentially.
- **Energy storage is the quiet winner.** $12.8B FY2025 revenue from battery storage (Megapack/Powerwall). Growing rapidly, high-margin, under-discussed.
- **AI compute investment.** $20B 2026 capex guided, heavily on FSD training, Optimus, and datacenter capacity.

**What the thesis should be honest about:**
- **The car business is shrinking.** FY2025 revenue fell to $94.8B from $97.7B. Q4 deliveries down 16% YoY. Full-year deliveries: 1.636M (declining). The EV thesis is dead — this is confirmed, not speculative.
- **Revenue declined year-over-year.** For a Growth-tier position, the company's total revenue went backwards. You're paying $420/share for a company with negative revenue growth.
- **Robotaxi is pre-revenue at scale.** 500 cars in 2 cities is a proof of concept, not a business. Revenue contribution is negligible. The thesis requires this to scale to millions of rides in dozens of cities.
- **Optimus is pre-everything.** Gen 3 is a prototype for future mass production. Revenue is zero. Commercial deployment is 2027+ at earliest. The 1M/yr target is aspirational.
- **$20B capex on bets, not the core business.** AI compute, robotaxi fleet, Optimus lines — all speculative ROI. If these bets don't pay off, that's $20B/yr in value destruction.
- **Brand/political risk is real.** Musk's DOGE involvement and political polarization are measurably affecting demand in some markets. European sales declining. This isn't noise — it's demand destruction in the car business you're relying on to fund the bets.
- **Cost basis $419.76 vs current $389.80 (-7%).** Underwater on a volatile, declining-revenue company.

## Invalidation Framework

### Early Warning (watch quarterly)
- Robotaxi expansion delays (miss H1 2026 city targets)
- FSD safety incidents triggering regulatory investigation
- Optimus Gen 3 demos show no meaningful capability improvement
- Auto gross margin ex-credits drops back below 16%
- Energy storage revenue growth decelerates below 20%
- European/China market share loss accelerates

### Invalidation Conditions
- **Robotaxi delayed beyond 2027** — if Level 4 autonomy doesn't scale to 10+ cities with paying customers by end of 2027, the premium valuation is unjustified
- **Robotics fails to reach production** — if Optimus doesn't ship units to external customers by end of 2027
- **Auto revenue decline accelerates** — if FY2026 auto revenue falls more than 10% YoY, the core business can't fund the bets
- **Brand damage causes sustained demand destruction** — if US demand (not just Europe/China) starts declining
- **Competition achieves robotaxi first at scale** — Waymo expanding faster than Tesla's robotaxi fleet

### What Would Make Me Add
- Robotaxi reaches 5+ cities with positive unit economics disclosed
- Optimus deployed in Tesla factories with measurable productivity gains
- Stock pulls back to $320 on macro/sentiment (not fundamental deterioration in robotaxi program)
- FSD achieves demonstrably lower accident rates than human drivers (regulatory validation)
- NOT: "Robotaxi launches in another city" alone — need evidence of unit economics

## Risk Notes

- **Cost basis $419.76 vs current $389.80 (-7%).** Underwater. At 51 shares, $21.4K cost — this is a venture-style bet inside a growth portfolio.
- **Revenue is declining.** This is the only Growth position where the company's revenue went backwards year-over-year.
- **The entire thesis is binary.** If robotaxi + Optimus work → stock could 3-5x. If they don't → stock should be $150-200 on EV fundamentals alone. There is no middle ground at this valuation.
- **Musk concentration risk.** CEO runs 6+ companies simultaneously (Tesla, SpaceX, xAI, X, Neuralink, DOGE). Time allocation to Tesla is a legitimate concern.
- **$389.80 stock price implies ~$1.2T market cap** for a company with $95B declining revenue. The market is pricing in robotaxi/Optimus success — any setback gets punished severely.
