## Thesis

SMB software monopoly (QuickBooks + TurboTax) with AI-driven mid-market expansion. The franchise is strong — high switching costs, recurring revenue, dominant share. The growth question is whether AI (Intuit Assist) can push QuickBooks upmarket into mid-market companies currently using NetSuite/Sage.

**What's working (Q2 FY2026 / Feb 2026 data):**
- **Revenue: $4.7B (+17% YoY).** Beat consensus ($4.53B). Non-GAAP EPS: $4.15 (+25%), beat by $0.49. Broad-based acceleration.
- **Global Business Solutions (QuickBooks): $3.2B (+18%).** Online Ecosystem revenue: $2.5B (+21%). This is the core growth engine and it's accelerating.
- **AI partnership with Anthropic.** Multi-year deal to integrate Claude into Intuit Enterprise Suite (IES). This directly targets the mid-market thesis — AI-powered accounting for 50-500 employee companies. This is exactly the catalyst the thesis identified.
- **TurboTax: $581M (+12%).** Steady, not explosive. Tax business is a cash cow with seasonal revenue.
- **~80% recurring/subscription revenue.** Highly predictable business model.

**What the thesis should be honest about:**
- **Full-year guidance: $21B revenue (+12-13%).** Solid but not growth-stock level. 12-13% growth at $431/share — is this a Growth-tier holding or a mature compounder? The growth rate is closer to MSFT/GOOG territory than PLTR/AFRM.
- **Cost basis $431.05 vs current $439.84 (+2%).** Essentially at cost. Zero margin of safety. A -10% pullback puts you underwater.
- **AI is both opportunity and threat.** If AI makes bookkeeping trivially easy (ChatGPT can already handle simple tax questions), Intuit's moat erodes. The counter-argument is that Intuit owns the data (your financial records) and the regulatory compliance layer (tax filing). But the threat is real for the simple end of the market.
- **IRS Direct File remains a wild card.** Government-provided free filing would undercut TurboTax's consumer business. Political dynamics are unpredictable.
- **Mid-market push is unproven.** The Anthropic partnership is announced, not delivered. IES needs to win customers from NetSuite/Sage, which have deeper ERP functionality. QuickBooks moving upmarket is a multi-year execution risk.
- **$15K position (35 shares) is mid-sized.** Appropriate for conviction level, but at +2% P&L there's no cushion.

## Invalidation Framework

### Early Warning (watch quarterly)
- Online Ecosystem revenue growth below 15% (currently 21%)
- Full-year revenue guidance below 10% (currently 12-13%)
- AI features get negative reception or low adoption metrics
- IRS Direct File gains meaningful consumer share (>5% of e-filings)
- Mid-market QuickBooks wins not disclosed after 4+ quarters of IES launch

### Invalidation Conditions
- **SMB spending contraction** — recession directly hits small business formation and software spending. If QuickBooks subscriber growth goes negative, the franchise is damaged.
- **AI disrupts the simple end** — if ChatGPT/free tools make basic bookkeeping and tax filing trivially easy, TurboTax consumer and QuickBooks Simple Start lose relevance
- **Mid-market push fails** — if IES doesn't gain traction after 2+ years, growth is capped by SMB TAM (~12% organic)
- **IRS Direct File scales** — government-provided free filing exceeding 10% of e-filings would undercut TurboTax economics

### What Would Make Me Add
- IES (Intuit Enterprise Suite) wins disclosed mid-market customers (50-500 employees)
- Online Ecosystem sustains 20%+ growth for 3+ quarters (proves AI is driving adoption)
- Stock pulls back to $380 on macro weakness
- Credit Karma monetization accelerates (disclosed separately and growing 20%+)
- NOT: "Intuit Assist shows product differentiation" — too vague. Need customer adoption metrics.

## Risk Notes

- **Cost basis $431.05 vs current $439.84 (+2%).** Essentially flat. No margin of safety.
- **Growth rate question.** At 12-13% guided revenue growth, this is a mature compounder, not a hypergrowth stock. The Growth tier is justified only if AI mid-market expansion materializes (pushing growth to 15-20%).
- **AI dual risk.** The same AI that could expand INTU's TAM (mid-market) could also shrink it (simple end commoditized). Net impact is uncertain.
- **Valuation.** ~35x forward earnings for a 12-13% grower. Not cheap, but not egregious for a monopoly franchise with 80% recurring revenue. The premium requires AI growth to materialize.
- **IRS Direct File.** Low probability of scaling quickly, but high impact if it does. Political risk — impossible to model.
