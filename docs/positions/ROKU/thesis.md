## Thesis

#1 connected TV platform in the US, monetizing through advertising as linear TV dollars migrate to streaming. Roku owns the aggregation layer — the home screen, ad insertion, and The Roku Channel — taking a cut of every ad dollar that flows through CTV. Hardware is a loss leader to grow the installed base; platform revenue is the real business.

**What's working (Q4 2025 / FY2025 data):**
- **Platform dominance is real.** 37-38% US CTV device market share (#1), 2x Amazon Fire TV (#2 at 17-18%). 90M+ streaming households globally, targeting 100M in 2026.
- **Platform revenue growing above market.** $1.224B in Q4 (+18% YoY) vs CTV ad market growing ~14%. Roku is gaining share of a growing market.
- **Profitability has arrived.** FY2025 net income: $88.4M (first profitable year, vs -$129M loss in 2024). Platform gross margin: 51-52%.
- **88% of revenue is high-margin platform.** The business model transformation from hardware company to ad platform is complete.
- **32% of open programmatic CTV ads** — #1 position in the ad tech layer.

**What's not working / missing from original thesis:**
- **ARPU is no longer disclosed.** Roku stopped reporting quarterly ARPU and streaming household metrics in detail. Original early warning was "ARPU growth stalls or declines" — this is uncheckable now. Governance gap.
- **Amazon Fire TV device share grew 65% YoY.** The biggest competitive threat is accelerating. Amazon can subsidize hardware indefinitely and bundle with Prime. Roku's share dipped from 38% to 37% in H1 2025.
- **Hardware losses are structural, not temporary.** Devices segment: negative mid-teens gross margin, guided to continue in 2026. Original thesis said "acceptable short-term to gain share" — this is permanent.
- **CTV ad growth is steady, not explosive.** $33.4B (2025) → $38B (2026) = 14% growth. This is a healthy secular trend, not a snapback recovery. The "ad market recovery" framing implies a catalyst that's already played out.
- **LG surged 79% QoQ in Q4 programmatic share.** Roku's 32% open programmatic share dipped 11% QoQ. Smart TV OS competitors (Samsung, LG) are native to the hardware — no separate device needed.

**The honest framing:** Roku is a well-executing #1 platform in a steadily growing market. The turnaround from losses to profitability is real. But the upside catalyst ("ad market recovery") has largely played out — what remains is steady mid-to-high teens growth, which is already priced into the stock at $91. The risk is erosion from below (Amazon subsidies, smart TV OS) rather than a sudden collapse.

## Invalidation Framework

### Early Warning (watch quarterly)
- Platform revenue growth decelerates below 15% (currently 18%)
- US CTV device market share drops below 33% (currently 37%)
- Amazon Fire TV share exceeds 25% (currently 17-18%, growing 65% YoY)
- Platform gross margin drops below 48% (currently 51-52%)
- Smart TV OS share (Samsung + LG combined) exceeds 25% (currently ~19%)
- Note: ARPU no longer disclosed — monitor revenue-per-household as proxy ($1.224B / 90M = ~$54/yr platform revenue per household)

### Invalidation Conditions
- **Platform share erosion** — US CTV share drops below 30% for 2+ consecutive quarters
- **Amazon breaks through** — Fire TV share exceeds 25% AND Roku share declining simultaneously
- **Platform revenue growth below market** — if Roku grows slower than the ~14% CTV ad market, they're losing, not winning
- **Content cost escalation** — if The Roku Channel requires significant content investment to maintain viewership (Netflix-level spend)
- **Hardware losses widen** — if Devices gross margin goes below -20% without offsetting platform growth

### What Would Make Me Add
- Platform revenue growth reaccelerates above 20% (sustained, not one quarter)
- ARPU disclosure resumes and shows acceleration
- Stock pulls back to $70-75 on macro (not market share loss)
- International streaming households hit an inflection (meaningful contribution outside US/Canada/Mexico)
- NOT: "ad market recovery" — this is already happening at 14% growth. Don't wait for a catalyst that's already here.

## Risk Notes

- **Beta 1.70** — 4th largest risk contributor in portfolio. $23K stress loss in a -30% SPY scenario.
- **Ad-cyclical business.** A recession directly hits CTV ad spending. The 2023-2024 ad softness dropped the stock from $300+ to $55. Revenue concentration in advertising = macro sensitivity.
- **Amazon's endgame.** Amazon doesn't need Fire TV to be profitable — it's a Prime acquisition channel. They can subsidize forever. If Fire TV reaches 25%+ share, Roku's platform premium erodes.
- **Smart TV OS threat.** Samsung and LG have native OS on their TVs. As smart TVs replace streaming sticks, the "separate device" moat weakens. Roku's counter is being the default OS on TCL/Hisense, but those are lower-end brands.
- **500 shares at $73 cost, currently $91 (+25%).** Reasonable unrealized gain but thin margin — a -20% pullback puts you near breakeven.
