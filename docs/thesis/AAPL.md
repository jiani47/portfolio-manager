# Apple Inc (AAPL)

**Tier:** Starter

## Thesis

Tracking position. Bet on AI application layer, not high conviction.

Apple has the distribution (2B+ active devices) and the privacy brand to be the AI application layer — the interface through which consumers interact with AI daily. Apple Intelligence is the bet. But unlike GOOG or NVDA, Apple isn't building frontier models or custom silicon for AI training. They're integrating others' models (OpenAI partnership) into their ecosystem.

Key pillars:
- **Ecosystem lock-in:** 2B+ active devices, high switching costs, services revenue growing steadily
- **Services margin:** App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, Apple TV+ — high-margin recurring revenue
- **Apple Intelligence potential:** If Siri + on-device AI actually works, it could drive a massive upgrade cycle
- **Capital return:** Largest buyback program in history, consistent dividend growth

## Invalidation Framework

### Early Warning
- iPhone upgrade cycle disappoints for 2+ quarters
- Apple Intelligence reviews are negative / low adoption
- China market share continues declining
- Services growth decelerates below 10%

### Invalidation Conditions
- **Apple Intelligence fails to drive upgrade cycle** — if AI features are a non-event and iPhone sales stagnate, the growth narrative breaks
- **China share loss accelerates** — Huawei and domestic competitors taking meaningful share would hurt the installed base story
- **Services antitrust** — if App Store fees are forced down significantly (EU DMA, US regulation), services margins compress
- **No proprietary AI moat** — if Apple remains dependent on OpenAI/Google for AI models, they're a distributor, not an innovator

### What Would Make Me Add
- Apple Intelligence drives measurable iPhone upgrade acceleration
- Siri becomes genuinely useful (competitive with ChatGPT/Gemini for daily tasks)
- Stock pulls back to $240 on macro weakness
- Proprietary AI model announcement

## Risk Notes

- Not high conviction — tracking position only. 40 shares, small allocation
- Cost basis ~$270 is near all-time highs — not a great entry
- The AI story is unproven. Apple is late to AI relative to Google, Meta, Microsoft
- Starter tier = still learning, not ready to size up
