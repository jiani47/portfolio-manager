## Thesis

Cash-equivalent parking. Short-duration treasury yield with near-zero risk.

SGOV is not an investment thesis — it's a cash management tool. Parked cash earns the short-term treasury rate instead of sitting idle. Ultra-short duration means near-zero interest rate risk.

## Invalidation Framework

### Exit Trigger
- **Rates drop to zero** — if the Fed cuts to zero, SGOV yield becomes negligible and cash might as well sit in a money market
- **Better cash deployment** — when there's a high-conviction buying opportunity, sell SGOV to fund it

## Risk Notes

- Essentially no risk — this is T-bills in ETF form
- Provides optionality: dry powder for market dislocations
- Monthly dividend distributions
