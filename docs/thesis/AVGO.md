## Thesis

ASIC leader — building custom silicon for hyperscalers who want alternatives to NVIDIA.

Broadcom is the #1 custom chip (ASIC) designer for hyperscalers. Google's TPU, Meta's MTIA, and other custom AI accelerators are designed with Broadcom. As hyperscalers diversify away from NVIDIA for inference workloads, Broadcom captures that shift. Plus VMware acquisition adds enterprise software recurring revenue.

Key pillars:
- **Custom silicon dominance:** Designs ASICs for Google, Meta, and others. The go-to partner when hyperscalers want custom AI chips
- **AI networking:** Tomahawk/Jericho switching silicon is critical infrastructure for AI data centers. You can't train large models without high-bandwidth networking
- **VMware acquisition:** Adds ~$12B in recurring enterprise software revenue. Margin expansion story as they optimize VMware's business
- **Diversified revenue:** Semiconductors + infrastructure software — not a pure-play chip company
- **Capital allocation:** Aggressive dividend growth, disciplined M&A track record

## Invalidation Framework

### Early Warning
- Hyperscaler custom silicon programs get delayed or scaled back
- AI networking share loss to competitors (Cisco, Arista)
- VMware integration issues or customer churn
- AI capex guidance cuts from major hyperscalers

### Invalidation Conditions
- **AI capex cycle peaks** — if hyperscalers cut back on AI infrastructure spending, both ASIC and networking revenue get hit
- **Loses ASIC share to Marvell** — Marvell is the #2 ASIC player. If they win key hyperscaler designs away from Broadcom, the moat weakens
- **VMware churn** — if the aggressive VMware repricing drives customers to alternatives (Nutanix, open-source), the software thesis breaks
- **Custom silicon doesn't scale** — if hyperscalers decide NVIDIA GPUs are good enough and abandon custom chip programs

### What Would Make Me Add
- New hyperscaler ASIC design win (beyond Google/Meta)
- VMware margins expand faster than expected
- Stock pulls back to $300 on broader semi sell-off
- AI networking revenue accelerates
