# Alibaba Group (BABA)

**Tier:** Growth
**Accounts:** Schwab 8819 (250 shares)
**Cost Basis:** $38,950 (~$155.80/share)
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-06

## Thesis

China e-commerce/cloud dominance + Qwen LLM makes this an AI play.

BABA is the Amazon of China — e-commerce, cloud (Alibaba Cloud #1 in China), logistics (Cainiao), payments (Ant Group stake). The Qwen LLM is quietly becoming one of the best open-source models globally, and they poached talent from Google DeepMind. If Qwen monetizes through Cloud AI workloads, this is undervalued on an AI-adjusted basis.

Key pillars:
- **E-commerce dominance:** Taobao + Tmall still #1 in China GMV despite PDD/JD competition
- **Cloud AI:** Alibaba Cloud is the default infrastructure for Chinese enterprises. Qwen models are competitive with GPT/Gemini/Claude on benchmarks
- **Valuation:** Trades at a massive discount to US peers. If sentiment on China tech shifts, re-rating potential is enormous
- **AI talent acquisition:** Poached DeepMind researchers — signal of serious AI ambition, not just marketing
- **Capital return:** Aggressive buybacks reducing share count

## Invalidation Framework

### Early Warning
- Qwen model development stalls (no new releases for 6+ months)
- Cloud revenue growth decelerates below 10%
- E-commerce market share loss to PDD accelerates
- Ant Group IPO permanently blocked

### Invalidation Conditions
- **Regulatory crackdown resumes** — new fines, forced restructuring, or government interference in operations
- **LLM monetization fails to materialize** — Qwen stays open-source with no cloud revenue uplift after 12+ months
- **E-commerce margin compression** — price war with PDD destroys unit economics
- **US delisting risk materializes** — ADR structure threatened by HFCAA or political action

### What Would Make Me Add
- Qwen demonstrates clear enterprise monetization (cloud AI revenue breakout)
- China stimulus drives consumer spending recovery
- Stock pulls back below $140 on macro noise (not fundamental deterioration)
- Ant Group IPO gets approved

## Risk Notes

- China regulatory risk is ever-present but has been de-escalating since 2023
- ADR structure adds a layer of risk vs direct ownership
- Currency risk: RMB weakness hurts USD-denominated returns
- Geopolitical tail risk (Taiwan scenario) would crater all China holdings
