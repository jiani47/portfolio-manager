## Thesis

Bet on agentic commerce — AI agents driving merchant transactions through Shopify's platform.

Shopify is the default e-commerce platform for independent merchants. The next catalyst is agentic commerce: AI agents that browse, compare, and buy on behalf of consumers. If agents become a meaningful commerce channel, they need an API-friendly, structured platform to transact on — and Shopify is best positioned to be that platform. This is speculative but high-upside.

Key pillars:
- **Merchant platform dominance:** #2 e-commerce platform in the US behind Amazon. Powers millions of merchants from SMB to enterprise (Shopify Plus)
- **Agentic commerce thesis:** AI agents need structured product catalogs, checkout APIs, and merchant trust. Shopify's infrastructure is built for this
- **Payments (Shop Pay):** Processing billions in GMV, growing faster than overall Shopify. High-margin fintech layer
- **International expansion:** Growing merchant base outside North America
- **Capital-light model:** Platform business, not inventory-heavy like Amazon

## Invalidation Framework

### Early Warning
- GMV growth decelerates below 15%
- Merchant churn increases (especially Shopify Plus)
- Agentic commerce remains theoretical with no real traction after 12+ months
- Amazon or other platforms capture the agent commerce layer

### Invalidation Conditions
- **Agentic commerce doesn't materialize** — if AI agents don't become a meaningful shopping channel in the next 2 years, the speculative premium evaporates
- **Loses to Shopify alternatives** — if platforms like BigCommerce, Wix, or Square capture meaningful share
- **Macro kills SMB merchants** — recession drives merchant closures and churn
- **Amazon captures agent commerce** — if AI agents default to Amazon's catalog instead of independent merchants

### What Would Make Me Add
- Stock returns to $120 support level (on dip-reentry watchlist)
- Clear evidence of AI agent commerce transactions through Shopify
- Shopify Plus wins accelerate (enterprise validation)
- Shop Pay GMV growth exceeds overall Shopify growth
