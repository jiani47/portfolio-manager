## Thesis

All-in-one fintech — loans, banking, trading, crypto, everything in one app.

SoFi is building the financial super-app for millennials/Gen-Z. Bank charter gives them deposit-taking ability (lower cost of capital vs fintech peers). The flywheel: acquire members via student loan refi → cross-sell banking, investing, credit cards, insurance. Member growth has been consistently strong.

Key pillars:
- **Bank charter moat:** One of very few fintechs with a full bank charter. Can hold deposits (cheaper funding) and lend directly
- **Member flywheel:** 10M+ members, growing 30%+ YoY. Each new product cross-sell increases LTV
- **Lending platform (Galileo/Technisys):** B2B fintech infrastructure — SoFi powers other fintechs and banks behind the scenes
- **Multiple revenue streams:** Lending, financial services (banking/investing), technology platform — diversifying away from pure lending risk
- **Rate environment:** Higher rates help net interest margin on the banking side

## Invalidation Framework

### Early Warning
- Member growth decelerates below 20% YoY
- Net interest margin compression from deposit competition
- Loan origination quality deteriorates (rising charge-offs)
- Galileo/Technisys loses major clients

### Invalidation Conditions
- **Loan losses spike** — if personal loan or student loan charge-offs exceed industry averages for 2+ quarters
- **Regulatory pressure on multi-product model** — forced to separate banking from brokerage or restrict product bundling
- **Member growth stalls** — below 15% YoY would signal the flywheel is broken
- **Deposit flight** — if members pull deposits to higher-yield alternatives, funding cost advantage disappears

### What Would Make Me Add
- Stock pulls back to $13-14 range on macro fear (not credit deterioration)
- Galileo wins a marquee enterprise client
- Cross-sell metrics improve (products per member increasing)
- Student loan refi volumes pick up if rates decline

## Risk Notes

- Fintech valuations are volatile — SOFI can swing 5-10% on any macro headline
- Concentrated in consumer lending — recession risk is real
- Competition from traditional banks (Chase, Goldman Marcus) and neobanks (Chime)
- Cost basis ~$15.29 — stock is volatile around this level
