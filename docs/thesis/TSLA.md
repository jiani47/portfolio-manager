## Thesis

Bet on robotics and autonomous driving, not the car business.

The EV business is mature and competitive. The thesis is that Tesla is actually a robotics/autonomy company that happens to sell cars. FSD (Full Self-Driving) and Optimus (humanoid robot) are the real bets. If robotaxi launches successfully and Optimus reaches commercial viability, the TAM is orders of magnitude larger than EVs. This is a high-conviction, high-risk position.

Key pillars:
- **FSD / Robotaxi:** If Tesla achieves Level 4+ autonomy at scale, the robotaxi business could be worth more than the entire current market cap
- **Optimus (humanoid robot):** Early-stage but Tesla has manufacturing scale, AI talent, and real-world training data from FSD
- **Manufacturing advantage:** Gigafactory network, vertically integrated battery production, casting innovation
- **Energy storage:** Megapack / Powerwall growing rapidly. Under-appreciated business line
- **Data moat:** Billions of miles of real-world driving data from the fleet — no competitor has this

## Invalidation Framework

### Early Warning
- Robotaxi launch delayed again beyond 2026
- FSD safety incidents that trigger regulatory scrutiny
- Optimus demos show no meaningful progress
- EV market share erosion accelerates (BYD, legacy OEMs)

### Invalidation Conditions
- **Robotaxi delayed indefinitely** — if Level 4 autonomy keeps slipping, the premium valuation is unjustified
- **Robotics fails to commercialize** — if Optimus remains a demo product with no path to revenue
- **Brand damage** — Musk's political activities causing sustained consumer backlash and demand destruction
- **Competition catches up on autonomy** — if Waymo, BYD, or others achieve robotaxi at scale first

### What Would Make Me Add
- Robotaxi launches in a real market with paying customers
- Optimus deployed in Tesla factories (proof of concept)
- Stock pulls back to $350 on macro/sentiment
- FSD achieves demonstrably lower accident rates than human drivers (regulatory validation)

## Risk Notes

- 51 shares at ~$420 — high cost basis, stock is volatile
- Musk risk: CEO distraction (xAI, X, SpaceX, DOGE) and political polarization affecting the brand
- Valuation assumes robotaxi/robotics success — if those fail, stock is massively overvalued on EV metrics alone
- EV competition intensifying globally, especially from BYD in China/Europe
