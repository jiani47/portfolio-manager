# Palantir Technologies (PLTR)

**Tier:** Growth
**Accounts:** Schwab 6196 (150 shares)
**Cost Basis:** $21,044 (~$140.29/share)
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-06

## Thesis

Leading AI/data platform for government + enterprise, with no real competitor at scale.

Palantir builds the operating system for data-driven decision making. Gotham (government) and Foundry (enterprise) are deeply embedded in customer workflows. AIP (Artificial Intelligence Platform) is the growth catalyst — it lets enterprises deploy LLMs on their own data with governance and security. Nobody else does this as well for large, complex organizations.

Key pillars:
- **Government lock-in:** Deep integration with US military, intelligence agencies, and allied governments. Switching costs are enormous — multi-year contracts with high renewal rates
- **AIP momentum:** AIP boot camps converting enterprise prospects at unprecedented rates. The "try before you buy" model is working
- **No real competitor:** The intersection of data integration + AI deployment + security clearance is a very narrow moat. Competitors either lack the clearance (startups) or lack the platform (consulting firms)
- **Rule of 40+:** Revenue growth + margin trending toward best-in-class SaaS metrics
- **US government AI spending:** Secular tailwind as DoD modernizes. Palantir is the default vendor

## Invalidation Framework

### Early Warning
- Enterprise deal velocity slows (AIP boot camps not converting)
- Government contract renewals come in below expectations
- Revenue growth decelerates below 20% for 2+ quarters
- Key executive departures (Karp/Sankar)

### Invalidation Conditions
- **Government budget cuts** — if defense/intelligence spending is meaningfully reduced, Gotham revenue at risk
- **Enterprise deals don't scale** — if AIP boot camps generate pilots but not production contracts, the enterprise thesis breaks
- **Valuation compression** — stock trades at a premium that requires sustained 25%+ growth. If growth slows to 15%, multiple contracts sharply
- **Competition emerges** — if a major cloud provider (AWS, Azure, GCP) builds a comparable platform with security clearances

### What Would Make Me Add
- Enterprise revenue crosses 50% of total (reduces government concentration risk)
- AIP shows clear evidence of multi-year enterprise contracts (not just pilots)
- Stock pulls back to $120 on macro weakness
- New major allied government contracts (NATO expansion)

## Risk Notes

- Valuation is rich — any growth miss gets punished severely
