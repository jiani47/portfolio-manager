## Thesis

#1 connected TV platform, ad market recovery play.

Roku owns the largest installed base of connected TV devices/OS in the US. The thesis is simple: linear TV ad dollars are migrating to streaming, and Roku is the gateway. They make money from platform revenue (ads shown on the Roku home screen, The Roku Channel, ad insertion on third-party apps). As the ad market recovers from the 2023-2024 softness, Roku captures an outsized share.

Key pillars:
- **Platform dominance:** #1 streaming platform by active accounts in the US. The default OS on many TV brands (TCL, Hisense)
- **Ad revenue secular trend:** Linear TV ad spend (~$60B) migrating to CTV. Roku is the aggregation layer
- **The Roku Channel:** Own content channel growing in viewership — higher-margin than hardware
- **International expansion:** Underpenetrated outside the US — long runway
- **Operating leverage:** Platform revenue is high-margin. As scale grows, margins should expand

## Invalidation Framework

### Early Warning
- Active account growth slows below 10% YoY
- ARPU growth stalls or declines
- Amazon Fire TV or Google TV gain meaningful platform share
- Ad market recovery takes longer than expected

### Invalidation Conditions
- **Streaming competition erodes device share** — if Amazon/Google/Apple bundle their way to platform dominance and Roku share drops below 30%
- **Ad market stays soft** — if CTV ad spending growth disappoints for 3+ quarters, the bull case timeline extends indefinitely
- **Hardware margin goes permanently negative** — acceptable short-term to gain share, but if it becomes structural, the business model is broken
- **Content costs escalate** — if The Roku Channel requires Netflix-level content spend to maintain viewership

### What Would Make Me Add
- Ad market shows clear recovery (industry-wide CTV spend accelerating)
- ARPU growth reaccelerates above 15%
- Stock pulls back to $65-70 on macro (not fundamental)
- International active accounts hit an inflection point

## Risk Notes

- 500 shares at ~$73 cost basis — meaningful position in Schwab 6196
- Ad-dependent business = cyclical. Macro downturn directly hits revenue
- Competition from tech giants (Amazon, Google, Apple) who can subsidize hardware
- Roku has been a volatile stock historically — needs conviction to hold through swings
